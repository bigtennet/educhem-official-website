About EduChem with Ajibola

Making Chemistry Exciting Again

Our Story

EduChem with Ajibola was born from a simple yet powerful belief: Chemistry doesn't have to be difficult or boring.

For too long, students have struggled with Chemistry, viewing it as a complex and intimidating subject. We saw the need to change this narrative and transform how Chemistry is taught and learned.

Through creativity, clarity, and connection, we've created a learning platform that makes Chemistry exciting, relatable, and accessible. Our approach combines storytelling, real-life applications, and digital visuals to simplify complex ideas and help students connect Chemistry to the world around them.

Today, EduChem serves as a central hub for Chemistry learning — where students, teachers, and science lovers can watch, learn, and engage with creative Chemistry content that inspires curiosity and builds confidence.

Meet the Founder

Ajibola Machado

Chemistry Educator | Science Communicator | Digital Learning Advocate

Ajibola Machado is a passionate Chemistry educator who has devoted his career to making Chemistry engaging and deeply relevant to students. He holds a B.Sc in Chemistry from the University of Benin and brings years of experience in science education and communication.

Through EduChem, Ajibola uses storytelling, real-life applications, and digital visuals to simplify complex ideas and help students connect Chemistry to the world around them. His teaching philosophy centers on:

  • Making learning relatable through real-world connections
  • Using creativity to make complex topics memorable
  • Building confidence through clear, concept-driven explanations
  • Inspiring curiosity and a love for science
  • Empowering students to excel in exams and beyond

Our Mission

To simplify Chemistry through creativity, clarity, and connection — helping students learn with understanding, curiosity, and confidence.

We believe that every student deserves access to quality Chemistry education that is engaging, accessible, and exam-focused. Our mission drives everything we do, from creating lesson content to developing exam preparation materials.

Our Vision

To become a leading science education platform in Africa that transforms how students learn and experience Chemistry — one engaging lesson at a time.

We envision a future where Chemistry education in Africa is innovative, accessible, and inspiring. A future where students don't just pass exams, but truly understand and appreciate the beauty of Chemistry.

Our Core Values

Passion

We are genuinely passionate about Chemistry and education, and it shows in everything we create.

Innovation

We continuously innovate our teaching methods and content to stay relevant and effective.

Accessibility

We believe quality education should be accessible to all students, regardless of their background.

Excellence

We strive for excellence in every lesson, resource, and interaction with our students.
